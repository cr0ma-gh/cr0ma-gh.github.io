Paint, ink and lay out comics.

Painter is a free painting app for illustration and comics, inspired by Clip Studio Paint and Photoshop. Bring your brushes, draw with a pen or the Apple Pencil, letter your pages, draw together with friends, and send CMYK files to print — on every platform you own.

Release 1.0 Milestone

Supercharged performance, legacy support & rolling preview

Painter 1.0 brings massive multiplatform speedups, low-spec PC optimization, enhanced brush integration, crash diagnostics, and our continuous Rolling Preview channel.

2.5× Faster SIMD & Parallel Canvas1.0

Instant 4K/8K flattening & responsive dabs

Integer SIMD vectorization (AVX2/NEON) and a dedicated multi-threaded worker pool make CPU canvas compositing up to 2.5× faster. Precomputed squared-distance dab rejection speeds up rasterization by 20–40% for silky-smooth painting even on dense textured brushes.

Rolling Preview Channel1.0

Test experimental features early

Want to try out cutting-edge capabilities before official releases? Painter now offers a continuous Rolling release channel. Switch effortlessly between Stable and Rolling Preview inside Help → Check for Updates, or grab daily builds straight from GitHub.

Legacy Hardware Profiles1.0

Smooth on budget & older PCs

Automatic detection for systems with low RAM (≤4 GB), integrated graphics, and downlevel texture limits. Choose between High Quality, Balanced, and Legacy profiles to paint without slowdowns.

Enhanced Brush Engine1.0

Cleaner UIX & full cross-compatibility

Streamlined brush settings with visual badges for spray, flat tips, and dual-tip textures. Flawless import and export of Clip Studio (.sut), Photoshop (.abr), Krita, MyPaint, and GIMP brushes.

Crash Diagnostics1.0

One-click GitHub issue reporting

In the rare event of an unexpected panic, an automatic crash reporter safely captures diagnostic details and lets you open a pre-filled GitHub bug report in one click.

Layer Content Previews1.0

Accurate artwork thumbnails

Layer thumbnails now intelligently zoom in on the tight bounding box of your actual strokes instead of shrinking the entire empty canvas, making it easy to identify layers at a glance.

Shared sessions

Draw together, like Drawpile — in the app

Host the open document and friends join with your address, on any platform. Strokes merge so two people painting in one spot both keep their paint; layers, texts and filters follow along. See everyone's pointer with their name, chat, and undo only your own work.

Text & balloonsNew

Letter your pages

Any font on your computer, vertical text for manga, outlines, and balloons — speech, thought, shout — with a tail you aim. Japanese and Chinese come from a fallback font when yours lacks them.

Features

Everything a page needs, from sketch to export

A full painting toolkit with the comic tools that usually cost extra, on a canvas drawn by your GPU.

Brushes

Brushes you already own

Imports Clip Studio .sut, Photoshop .abr, Krita .kpp/.bundle, MyPaint and GIMP brushes — and exports them back. Image tips, dual tips, textures, blend modes, tilt, speed and pressure.

Layers

Layers like the pros

Raster, vector, text, folders, paper, gradients, screentones and file objects, with 13 blend modes, masks, clipping, locks and draft layers. Vector lines you can reshape point by point.

Comics

Comic pages

Print presets from manga B5 to webtoon strips, bleed and gutters, panel templates and a frame tool. Each panel is a masked folder, so the art stays inside the border.

Rulers

Rulers & perspective

Perspective rulers with 1–3 vanishing points, symmetry, parallel, radial and concentric rulers, smart shapes when you hold the pen still, and straight lines with Shift.

3D

3D references, painted

Drop in .blend, FBX, glTF or OBJ models, pose rigged characters with IK, use their camera for perspective, and paint textures on the model or its UV map.

Files

Opens your files

Clip Studio .clip, Photoshop PSD, Krita KRA, OpenRaster and PNG — with layers, folders, masks and blend modes. Export to PSD (RGB or CMYK), KRA, ORA, CLIP, PNG and SVG.

References

Reference board

A PureRef-style board beside your canvas: drop images, videos and whole documents, arrange, scale and draw notes over them.

Scanning

Scan your pencils

Import from Wi-Fi scanners (AirScan), USB scanners on Windows or the iPad camera, then crop, straighten and clean up line art into layers.

Tablets

Pen-first on tablets

WinTab and Windows Ink pressure and tilt; on iPad the Pencil draws while fingers pan, zoom, rotate and undo, with a brush preview while it hovers. Workspaces, themes and panels are yours to arrange.

A calm workspace that gets out of the way

New · Shared sessions

Two artists, one page

The host shares the open document from File → Shared Session; a friend joins and adds the lettering while you paint. Their balloons, layers and strokes arrive as they make them.

Works between Windows, Mac, Linux, iPad and Android on the same network, or over the internet with the port forwarded or a VPN such as Tailscale.

New · Text & balloons

Lettering that stays editable

Click to write, or drag a balloon and type in it. Change the font, size, spacing, outline or balloon shape at any time; drag the tail's tip to aim it.

New · CMYK

See it as it will print

Convert with the press profile your printer asks for. Painter finds the profiles already on your computer (Adobe's, Windows', macOS'), or load any .icc.

New · Colour modes

Halftone in one step

Bitmap mode turns a picture into pure black and white — a 50% threshold, a diffusion dither, or a halftone screen with the cell size and angle you choose, ready for manga printing.

New · Channels

One channel at a time

Show red, green or blue alone, paint only in them, or load one as a selection. Panels stack as tabs — Layers, Channels, History — to leave the canvas more room.

New · Compact interface

Fewer buttons, same tools

Tool families share one button, as in Photoshop: hold it or right-click for the others. Brush settings fit on one screen, one row per setting.

3D layers

3D layers you can paint

Models sit on their own layer, lit and depth-tested on the GPU. Orbit with the middle button like in Blender, pose rigged characters, then frame the camera for your panel.

Give an object a texture and paint it with any brush, on the model or on its UV map.

iPad

Install on iPad by sideloading

Painter isn't on the App Store. You sign it yourself with a free Apple ID — it takes about ten minutes the first time.

  1. Download the iPad buildPainter-ipad-unsigned.ipa from the latest release.
  2. Install Sideloadly on your computerFrom sideloadly.io (Windows or Mac). On Windows also install iTunes from apple.com — not the Microsoft Store version.
  3. Connect the iPadPlug it in with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust on the iPad.
  4. Sign and installDrag the .ipa into Sideloadly, type your Apple ID and press Start. Sideloadly signs the app with your account and installs it.
  5. Trust your Apple ID on the iPadSettings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
  6. Turn on Developer ModeOn iPadOS 16 and later: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, then restart the iPad and confirm.

Good to know

  • With a free Apple ID the signature lasts 7 days. Reinstall with Sideloadly (it can refresh automatically over Wi-Fi) to keep using it — your files stay.
  • A paid Apple Developer account gives one-year signatures.
  • AltStore works the same way if you prefer it to Sideloadly.

Painting on iPad

  • The Apple Pencil draws, with a brush preview while it hovers; one or two fingers pan, pinch zooms and rotates.
  • Two-finger tap undoes, three-finger tap redoes, four-finger tap hides the panels.
  • On-screen Ctrl Shift Alt keys work with every shortcut and tool; the clock and battery chip shows the time Painter's full screen hides.
  • Files open from the Files app, iCloud Drive or other apps; Painter keeps its own folder under On My iPad.

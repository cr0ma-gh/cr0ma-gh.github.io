Free & open source · Rust · GPU canvas

Paint, ink and lay out comics.

Painter is a painting app for illustration and comics, inspired by Clip Studio Paint. Bring your brushes from Clip Studio, Photoshop, Krita and MyPaint, draw with a pen or the Apple Pencil, and work on every platform you own.

Features

Everything a page needs, from sketch to export

A full painting toolkit with the comic tools that usually cost extra, on a canvas drawn by your GPU.

Brushes you already own

Imports Clip Studio .sut, Photoshop .abr, Krita .kpp/.bundle, MyPaint and GIMP brushes — and exports them back. Image tips, dual tips, textures, tilt, speed and pressure dynamics.

Layers like the pros

Raster, vector, folders, paper, gradients and screentones, with 13 blend modes, masks, clipping, locks and draft layers. Multi-select, drag, group and change many at once.

Comic pages

Print presets from manga B5 to webtoon strips, bleed and gutters, panel templates and a frame tool. Each panel is a masked folder, so the art stays inside the border.

Rulers & perspective

Perspective rulers with 1–3 vanishing points, symmetry, parallel, radial and concentric rulers, and straight lines with Shift on every brush.

3D references, painted

Drop in .blend or .obj models with Blender-style navigation, use their camera for perspective, and paint textures right on the model or on its UV map.

Opens your files

Clip Studio .clip, Photoshop PSD, Krita KRA, OpenRaster and PNG — with layers, folders, masks and blend modes. Export to PSD, KRA, ORA, CLIP, PNG and SVG.

Reference board

A PureRef-style board beside your canvas: drop images and whole documents, arrange, scale and draw notes over them.

Scan your pencils

Import from Wi-Fi scanners (AirScan), USB scanners on Windows or the iPad camera, then crop, straighten and clean up line art into layers.

Pen-first on tablets

WinTab and Windows Ink pressure and tilt; on iPad the Pencil draws while fingers pan, zoom, rotate and undo. Workspaces, themes and panels are all yours to arrange.

Screenshots

A calm workspace that gets out of the way

Layers, folders and blend modes

Every layer with its blend mode and opacity at a glance, folders you can fold, and the colour wheel always close.

Hide the bars you don't need, float any panel in its own window, and switch between workspaces for painting, inking, 3D layout and comics.

3D layers you can paint

Models sit on their own layer, lit and depth-tested on the GPU. Orbit with the middle button like in Blender, then pose the camera for your panel.

Give an object a texture and paint it with any brush, on the model or on its UV map with a live preview.

How to install ↓

Older versions and release notes are on the releases page. Builds aren't code-signed by a paid certificate yet, so your system may warn the first time.

iPad

Install on iPad by sideloading

Painter isn't on the App Store. You sign it yourself with a free Apple ID — it takes about ten minutes the first time.

  1. Download the iPad buildPainter-ipad-unsigned.ipa from the latest release.
  2. Install Sideloadly on your computerFrom sideloadly.io (Windows or Mac). On Windows also install iTunes from apple.com — not the Microsoft Store version.
  3. Connect the iPadPlug it in with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust on the iPad.
  4. Sign and installDrag the .ipa into Sideloadly, type your Apple ID and press Start. Sideloadly signs the app with your account and installs it.
  5. Trust your Apple ID on the iPadSettings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
  6. Turn on Developer ModeOn iPadOS 16 and later: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, then restart the iPad and confirm.

Good to know

  • With a free Apple ID the signature lasts 7 days. Reinstall with Sideloadly (it can refresh automatically over Wi-Fi) to keep using it — your files stay.
  • A paid Apple Developer account gives one-year signatures.
  • AltStore works the same way if you prefer it to Sideloadly.

Painting on iPad

  • The Apple Pencil draws; one or two fingers pan, pinch zooms and rotates.
  • Two-finger tap undoes, three-finger tap redoes, four-finger tap hides the panels.
  • On-screen Ctrl Shift Alt keys work with every shortcut and tool.
  • Files open from the Files app, iCloud Drive or other apps; Painter keeps its own folder under On My iPad.
